Before the camera turns on

  • Camera only — the microphone is never opened.
  • Nothing is recorded. Video is processed in memory and never shown, saved, or sent anywhere. Only numbers (your pulse and breathing rate) are used.
  • No account, no cloud, no storage. Close the app and nothing remains.
  • SDK telemetry is off. Set explicitly to enableTelemetry: false.
  • Not a medical device. It shows measurements; it never judges your emotional or medical state.
